# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Shop teams responsible for keeping stockroom inventory current. Their core job is to maintain an accurate product list as stock is added, updated, or removed.

## Product Purpose

A web inventory-management application that lets shops track the products in their stockrooms. Success means staff can reliably add products and remove products that are no longer stocked.

## Positioning

Focused on the practical day-to-day maintenance of a shop's stockroom product inventory.

## Operating Context

Used by shop teams while managing their stockrooms and product records.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product inventory tracking.
- Add products.
- Delete products.
- Other product fields, stock-count behavior, user roles, permissions, and business rules are undecided.

## Evidence on Hand

- Angular frontend scaffold at `crud-app-frontend/`.
- A placeholder Products feature exists, but there is no established product UI, brand system, or supplied content/assets.

## Product Principles

- Keep routine inventory maintenance direct and understandable.
- Make the current product list easy for shop teams to trust.
- Support accurate stockroom records without adding needless complexity.
